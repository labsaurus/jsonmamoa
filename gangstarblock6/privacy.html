GANGSTAR BLOCK 6

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026.

Gangstar Block 6 (“we”, “the app”) is an open-world action game set in a fictional city, made for players aged 13 and up. This policy explains what data the app uses.

1. No account

You do not need to register or sign in. Your character name, skins, weapons, story progress, cash, turf, homes and settings are stored on your device only.

2. On-device data

Game progress is saved locally on your device. We do not send it to our own servers. Uninstalling the app or using Settings → RESET PROGRESS deletes it.

3. Ads

The app is free and supported by ads (Google AdMob and Appodeal with their ad partners: interstitial, rewarded and app-open ads). Ad partners may collect and use your device's advertising ID, IP address, device information and ad interactions to show, measure and personalize ads, under their own privacy policies. Where the law requires it, you are asked for consent before ads are personalized, and you can change your choice any time in Settings → PRIVACY CHOICES. We do not sell your personal data.

4. Remote settings

The app downloads a small settings file (ad switches) from the internet. It contains no data about you.

5. Notifications

If you allow them, the app sets local reminders (for example, when your daily gift is ready). They are scheduled on your device; no data is sent.

6. No purchases

The app sells nothing. Cash, skins and weapons are in-game items earned by playing or by watching optional ads; they have no real-world value and cannot be exchanged for money.

7. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. Ads are not configured as child-directed.

8. Changes

This policy may be updated. The latest version is always shown on this screen.

Contact: through the developer contact on the app's Google Play Store listing.
